What to do next

Short answers for boarding and Lightning. Balances live in your wallet data; this page is documentation only.

Day-one path

  1. Deposit on-chain XBT to your deposit address.
  2. Wait for the ASP confirmation count (often 3).
  3. Board less than your full balance — fees and recovery reserves are extra.
  4. After the board confirms, Overview shows an Ark balance.

Boarding tips

Board amount is not “balance including fees.” Leave sats behind. While a board is in flight, Overview Mid-flight shows pending board and unconfirmed change — a temporary confirmed 0 does not mean funds vanished.

Lightning receives

Creating an invoice does not move funds. State stays awaiting-payment until a Blake2b LN payer settles the bolt11 and this wallet finishes the Ark claim. Open invoices are listed on the Lightning tab. Keep the wallet service running.

If a pay attempt never settles, the ASP CLN hold plugin may be rejecting Blake2b invoice features — that is infra, not a missing UI row.

Safety

Experimental software. Backup before funding. Use only amounts you can afford to lose.
